## ⚠ Three things to get right, because this episode breaks the pattern

**1. The time is 6:30 pm, not the usual door.** Every other entry on the site is
a standard Nerd Nite evening. **Regulars will assume the usual time and arrive
late.** The time has to be prominent in the body, not only on the poster — a
poster is an image and images do not get read carefully.

**2. Tickets are on Eventbrite, not Humanitix.** Every existing ticket link on
the site goes to Humanitix. This one is Jennie's listing, so Eventbrite is
correct — but it will look like a mistake to anyone checking, and a muscle-memory
"fix" to Humanitix would break it.

**3. The title does not match the ticket page.** The poster says **"Singles
Night"**; Eventbrite says **"Nerd Nite Fort Collins Presents: Singles Event."**
The copy above uses the poster wording, so a reader clicking through lands on a
page titled slightly differently. Harmless if deliberate, confusing if not.
Pick one and know which.
